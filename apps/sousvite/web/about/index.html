Sousvite

Recipes, accurate nutrition, grocery lists, and a cooking mode in one app.

Sousvite is a recipe app for iOS, Android, and the web. Save recipes from any website or write your own, see their nutrition per batch and per serving, add what you need to a grocery list, and cook with amounts shown right in each step, in the measuring cups and spoons you own.

Open Sousvite on the web

What it does

  • Import and write recipes. Import from a link or from Umami, or type a recipe and Sousvite splits amounts from ingredients.
  • Accurate nutrition. Ingredients are matched to USDA FoodData Central, with Open Food Facts for packaged foods.
  • Grocery lists. Add a recipe's ingredients to your list, choosing which ones you need.
  • Cooking mode. Step by step, with each step's amounts inline, so you don't flip back and forth.
  • Experiments. Try changes to a recipe and keep its history.

Your data

Sousvite works without an account, and your recipes stay on your device. Sign in with Google or Apple to back up your library, sync it between devices, and share recipes. If you sign in with Google, you can sync through your own Google Drive instead of our servers. There are no ads, and we never sell your data. The privacy policy has the details.
