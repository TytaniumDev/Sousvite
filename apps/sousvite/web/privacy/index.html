Privacy policy

Sousvite · Effective 18 November 2026

Sousvite is a recipe app for iOS, Android, and the web. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. The short version: your recipes are yours, the app works without an account, and we never sell your data or show ads.

Using Sousvite without an account

You don't need to sign in. Without an account, your recipes, folders, grocery lists, measuring tools, and settings are stored only on your device. We don't receive any of it.

Signing in

You can sign in with Google or Apple to back up your recipes, sync between devices, and share recipes. Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication, a Google service. Google or Apple shares your account id, name, email address, and (for Google) profile picture with us. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we receive the private relay address instead. We use these only to run your account, sync, and sharing.

Sousvite cloud sync and backup

When you're signed in, your library syncs through Sousvite cloud by default. Our servers run on Google Cloud (Firebase) in the United States. We store your recipes, folders, grocery lists, experiments, recipe history, and photos there so they're backed up and can reach your other devices. During the beta this is free for everyone.

Sync through your Google Drive

If you sign in with Google, you can choose to sync through your own Google Drive instead. The app then asks for access to its own hidden app folder only (the drive.appdata permission). It can't see, read, or change any of your other Drive files. While Drive sync is on, your library and photos are stored in that folder, in your Drive, and not on our servers.

Sharing

  • When you share a recipe or folder with someone, they can see what you shared, and your name and email address are shown to them so they know who shared it.
  • When you create a view-only link to a recipe, anyone with the link can see that recipe. You can turn the link off at any time.

Subscriptions

There are no paid plans during the beta. If we add a Pro subscription later, purchases will go through the App Store, Google Play, or Stripe on the web, and we'll use RevenueCat to check which subscription you have. We'll receive your subscription status and an anonymous purchase id, never your card or bank details. We'll update this policy before that happens.

Nutrition lookups and recipe import

  • Common foods are looked up in a database built into the app, so those lookups don't leave your device.
  • Searches for branded foods are sent to the USDA FoodData Central service (through our server) and to Open Food Facts. They receive the search words or barcode, not who you are.
  • When you import a recipe from a link, the app downloads that web page. On the web version, the download goes through our server because browsers block it otherwise. We don't keep the pages or the links you import.

App security checks

To stop other apps from using our servers, Sousvite uses Firebase App Check. It asks Apple (App Attest), Google Play (Play Integrity), or Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise on the web to confirm that requests come from a genuine copy of the app. Those services receive technical signals about your device or browser under their own privacy policies.

Crash reports

When the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a report to Sentry so we can fix the bug. Reports contain technical details such as the error, the app version, and the device model and operating system. They don't include your recipes, your name, or your email address.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your data.
  • We don't show ads, use advertising ids, or track you across other apps and websites.
  • We don't use analytics services.
  • Sousvite has no AI features, and we don't use your recipes to train AI models.

Google user data

Sousvite's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google data only to sign you in and to sync your Sousvite data. We don't transfer it to anyone else except as needed to provide those features, to comply with the law, or with your permission, and no person at Sousvite reads it unless you ask us to for support.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Data on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • You can delete your account in the app's Settings. This deletes your account and everything we store on our servers for it. A copy stays on your device. Recipes you shared jointly stay with the other people in the share.
  • Data in your Google Drive app folder is yours to keep or remove. You can remove it from Google Drive's settings under Manage apps.
  • You can also ask us to delete your data by email (below). We'll do it within 30 days.

Children

Sousvite is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. If a change affects how we use data you've already given us, we'll tell you in the app first.
